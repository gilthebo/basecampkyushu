Kyūshū at a Glance

Seven prefectures, each with its own character

九州七県の特色

Fukuoka

福岡

Kyūshū's gateway city blends modern urban energy with pristine coastal scenery. Home to Itoshima's beaches and rural charm alongside Fukuoka City's metropolitan vibrancy.

Saga

佐賀

Japan's ceramic heartland featuring Arita and Imari pottery traditions. Ancient sake breweries, historic shrines, mountain kilns, and therapeutic hot springs define this culturally rich prefecture.

Nagasaki

長崎

International trading port with centuries of cultural fusion. Dramatic harbor cityscapes, remote islands, Dutch-influenced architecture, and profound historical significance.

Kumamoto

熊本

Dominated by Mount Aso, one of the world's largest active calderas. Majestic castle ruins, samurai history, volcanic landscapes, and pristine mountain water sources.

Oita

大分

Japan's hot spring capital boasting more onsen sources than any other prefecture. Rugged coastlines, geothermal "hells," mountain resort towns, and therapeutic waters throughout.

Miyazaki

宮崎

Pacific-facing prefecture known for world-class surf breaks and ancient mythology. Subtropical climate, palm-lined beaches, legendary shrines, and the birthplace of Japan's creation myths.

Kagoshima

鹿児島

Southern frontier crowned by Sakurajima, one of Japan's most active volcanoes. Tropical islands, samurai heritage, unique shochu distilleries, and the gateway to Yakushima's ancient forests.
