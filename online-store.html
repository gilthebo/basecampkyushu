The Shirt

着 物 帯

A contemporary kimono-collar top with a horizontal band of genuine vintage obi or kimono fabric across the chest — sourced and selected in Kyūshū. Linen or cotton base. A relaxed silhouette that reads as fashion, not costume.

Each edition is named for its fabric. When the fabric runs out, the edition closes permanently.
